SPX volatility surface and stochastic-volatility calibration

Arbitrage-free SVI, Heston via Carr–Madan FFT, SABR, daily recalibration
  • Built a pipeline on end-of-day SPX option chains (about 30,000 contracts a day, 103 trading days) that extracts forwards and discount factors from put-call parity and inverts roughly 1,600 implied vols a day.

Point-in-time data, pre-specified signals, cost-aware portfolio tests
  • Built a point-in-time US equity database on CRSP, Compustat and I/B/E/S (2.5M stock-months, 1985–2025), aligning fundamentals to announcement dates and correcting split, fiscal-year and delisting-return errors.
